# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in OpenWorkSpace a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for differing viewpoints, constructive feedback, accountability, and focusing on the community's best interests.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing private information without permission, and sustained disruption.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions that are not aligned with this Code and take fair corrective action in response to harmful behavior.

Adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
